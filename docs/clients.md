# Target Enterprise Prospects for Q4 2026 Mandate

## Scoring Methodology: Necessity Score (1.0 to 10.0)

The **Necessity Score** measures how urgently a company requires an external e-invoicing middleware to avoid business disruption, customer invoice rejection, and statutory audit penalties:

- **9.5 – 10.0 (Critical / Immediate Risk):** Multi-depot operations with high transaction volume, daily returns/credit notes (Schedule 04 immutability pain), and no internal software developers.
- **9.0 – 9.4 (High Urgency):** B2B supplier selling to corporate/supermarket chains demanding verified Gazette 2481/22 serials (`YYMMM_QQQQ_XXXXX`) to claim Schedule 02 input VAT.
- **8.0 – 8.9 (Elevated Operational Pain)y:** High item-count batches, mixed tax rates, or strict regulatory dependencies requiring clean digital reporting without in-house tech teams.
